## Problem

Grayscale or faded imagery of produce loses the color cues buyers and downstream vision systems rely on. This work investigates learning true-to-color reconstruction for white-background fruits and vegetables imagery.

## Method

The paper presents WBFVD, a U-Net-based deep learning model for the task. Architecture, dataset splits, and evaluation below reflect only the publication record; an author-approved technical summary is pending.
